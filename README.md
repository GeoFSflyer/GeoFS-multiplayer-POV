GeoFS Multiplayer Spectator POV

A browser userscript that adds a **Watch** button beside pilots in GeoFS's multiplayer list and a small spectator panel. It lets you watch a selected multiplayer aircraft from several local camera positions without using GeoFS's **Join** action.

## What it does

- Adds **Watch** beside each pilot in the multiplayer player list.
- Also provides a pilot selector and camera selector in a small floating panel.
- Camera choices: **Rear chase**, **Front view**, **Left side**, **Right side**, **Overhead**, and **Pilot POV (approx.)**.
- Keeps your own aircraft where it is; it does not call GeoFS's join/teleport action or change your flight controls.
- **Stop** restores your camera view from before spectating. If the selected pilot leaves, the addon stops and restores it automatically.
